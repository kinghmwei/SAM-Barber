About the shop

Sam Barber, known online as @sam_dubaibarber, is a men's hair and grooming specialist based in Dubai. The shop works as a modern gents salon and luxury grooming concept, built around detailed hair transformations.

On TikTok and Instagram, Sam has grown a following through satisfying before-and-after transformations, precision skin fades, hair flows and styling tailored to what's trending — and through clients who simply tell their friends.
